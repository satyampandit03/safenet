Safety Protocols

Standard operating procedures for handling digital threats.

📸 Secure Evidence First

▼
Before blocking or deleting anything, take a screenshot. This is your immutable proof. Save the date and time. On Android: Volume Down + Power. On iPhone: Side button + Volume Up. Store these locally.

🚫 Block & Report Protocol

▼
Always report AND block. Reporting pushes the platform's moderation algorithms to take action on the malicious account. On Instagram/WhatsApp/Snapchat: Tap the profile, hit the three dots, and execute Block/Report.

🔒 Lockdown Privacy

▼
Audit your privacy settings immediately. Set Instagram/Snapchat to Private. On WhatsApp, restrict Profile Photo and Status to "My Contacts". Never broadcast live location data publicly.
